## 1. The service

Re:Read is a personal, on-device reading companion. You register books, save passages from them
(by typing, pasting, or reading text off a photo of a page), add your own thoughts and tags, meet
those passages again on a spaced-repetition schedule, turn some of them into practices you check
off, and see basic statistics. Review reminders are scheduled by your own device.

The App has **no user accounts, no login and no cloud sync**. Every feature is free of charge; the
